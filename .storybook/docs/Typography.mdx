# Typography

Two families, split by job. Area Normal carries anything structural — headings, labels,
buttons, counts — and always uppercase. DM Sans carries everything a person actually reads in
sentences.

The split is what gives the app its voice, and it is easy to break by accident: a heading set in
the body font reads as a paragraph, and a paragraph set in Area uppercase becomes unreadable
at the length guests are asked to read.

## Headings — `--font-heading`

Area Normal ExtraBold (800), with Black (900) available for stronger emphasis. Sizes are set per context rather than by a global scale,
because a heading on the guest hero and a heading on an admin section are doing different jobs.

<Story of={Specimens.HeadingScale} />

App headings use `--tracking-heading` and labels use `--tracking-label`, defined in
`src/styles/base.css`. These tighter settings reduce wrapping with Area’s wide letterforms;
Arabic and Farsi reset them to zero to preserve joined letters. Brand lockups and document
headings keep their own spacing.

## Body — `--font-body`

DM Sans, sentence case, weight 400. Line height sits between 1.5 and 1.65 — generous, because a
large share of guests are reading a translation of a second language on a phone outdoors.

<Story of={Specimens.BodyScale} />

## Numbers

One number in the app is set large on purpose: the guest's place in the queue. It is Area Normal at
44px in the brand color, and it is the only thing on the screen at that size, so a guest can check
their position at arm's length without reading anything else.

## Rules of thumb

- Headings, buttons, labels, and counts use `--font-heading` and are uppercase.
- Anything longer than a few words uses `--font-body` in sentence case.
- Never set body copy in uppercase. Arabic and Farsi have no case at all, so uppercase styling
  silently does nothing there — a distinction that exists only in English is not a distinction the
  design can rely on.
- Both primary families are self-hosted as WOFF2 through `public/fonts/brand.css`, using
  `font-display: swap`. Area falls back to DM Sans; DM Sans falls back to Roboto, then Arial
  and the system sans-serif. Noto Sans Arabic and Noto Sans SC cover script-specific fallbacks.
- Area ships in weights 800 and 900; requests for lighter heading weights use the supplied 800
  face. DM Sans has static weights 400–900 and a regular italic face.
- The app preloads the regular body face and ExtraBold heading face. Presentations embed their
  fonts so standalone pages do not need the app’s asset server.
