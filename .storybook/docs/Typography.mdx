# Typography

Two families, split by job. Roboto Condensed carries anything structural — headings, labels,
buttons, counts — and always uppercase. Roboto carries everything a person actually reads in
sentences.

The split is what gives the app its voice, and it is easy to break by accident: a heading set in
the body font reads as a paragraph, and a paragraph set in Condensed uppercase becomes unreadable
at the length guests are asked to read.

## Headings — `--font-heading`

Roboto Condensed, uppercase, weight 700. Sizes are set per context rather than by a global scale,
because a heading on the guest hero and a heading on an admin section are doing different jobs.

<Story of={Specimens.HeadingScale} />

App headings use `--tracking-heading` and labels use `--tracking-label`, defined in
`src/styles/base.css`; Arabic and Farsi reset them to zero to preserve joined letters.

## The Brand Name — `--font-brand`

The market's name in the app bar, and the brand lockup on documents, are set in the brand kit's
Area Normal ExtraBold (800). Nothing else in the app is. Area was briefly the heading font too, and
went back to Roboto Condensed: its wide letterforms pushed headings, labels, and buttons out of
their containers, most of all in translation.

## Body — `--font-body`

Roboto, sentence case, weight 400. Line height sits between 1.5 and 1.65 — generous, because a
large share of guests are reading a translation of a second language on a phone outdoors.

<Story of={Specimens.BodyScale} />

## Numbers

One number in the app is set large on purpose: the guest's place in the queue. It is Condensed at
44px in the brand color, and it is the only thing on the screen at that size, so a guest can check
their position at arm's length without reading anything else.

## Rules of thumb

- Headings, buttons, labels, and counts use `--font-heading` and are uppercase.
- Anything longer than a few words uses `--font-body` in sentence case.
- Never set body copy in uppercase. Arabic and Farsi have no case at all, so uppercase styling
  silently does nothing there — a distinction that exists only in English is not a distinction the
  design can rely on.
- Roboto (400, 500, 700) and Roboto Condensed (500, 600, 700) are loaded from Google Fonts in
  `base.css`, then fall back to Arial and the system sans-serif. Area is self-hosted as WOFF2
  through `public/fonts/brand.css`, using `font-display: swap`, and falls back to Roboto. Noto Sans Arabic and Noto Sans SC cover
  script-specific fallbacks.
- Area ships in weights 800 and 900.
- Body text was briefly set in the brand kit's DM Sans and went back to Roboto, which reads better
  at small sizes and fits translated strings that DM Sans pushed out of their containers. Its
  files and `@font-face` rules are still in `public/fonts/`, unused, so it is never downloaded.
- The app preloads Area's ExtraBold face for the app bar. Presentations embed their
  fonts so standalone pages do not need the app’s asset server.
