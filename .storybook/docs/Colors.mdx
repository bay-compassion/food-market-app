# Colors

Every value below is read live from `src/styles/base.css` by the React component behind these tables.
The palette is deliberately small: Harbor Blue carries the primary interface, and
everything else is either a neutral or a signal.

## Brand

Harbor Blue (`#275C63`) fills the top bar, guest hero, primary buttons, and queue-position number.
White is the contrast color on Harbor Blue. Lake Blue (`#7EBDC5`) supplies secondary accents with
dark text; black is the primary text color on white. Hover and soft surface shades are derived
from this palette. Functional status colors and the amber focus ring keep their existing roles.

## Text

Three weights of neutral, in descending prominence. Reach for the darkest by default and step down
only for genuinely secondary content — this app is used outdoors, on phones, often by people
reading a second language.

## Notes on use

**The focus ring is not optional.** `base.css` puts a 3px `--color-focus` outline on every input,
button, and link at `:focus-visible`. It is amber rather than the brand color so it stays visible
against the brand-filled top bar. Do not override it in a component.

**`--color-error` does double duty.** It marks failure, but it also marks the "called to the
counter" state on the guest card — the one moment the app needs to interrupt someone who is not
looking at their phone. That is deliberate, not a misuse, and it is the reason the called state
also changes the icon and the heading rather than relying on color alone.

**`--color-success` and `--color-warning` exist only for `Alert`.** They stay out of the
`info` and `error` severities on purpose: `info` reuses `--color-brand` and `error` reuses
`--color-error`, so the palette only grows for the two signals nothing else in the app already
carries. Neither is safe as a background with white text at these values — they're tuned to pass
4.5:1 as text and icon-badge fills on `--color-background` and `--color-surface-soft`, not as full
panel fills.

**Contrast.** `--color-text-subtle` on `--color-background` is the lightest pairing in the app, at
roughly 5:1. That clears the 4.5:1 floor for body text but not by much, so do not lighten it
further, and do not use it on `--color-surface-soft` for anything a guest has to read.
