Set Up the Stations

The numbers on the plan show where each one goes.

1

Check-In

Lobby, by the restrooms

  1. 1

    Roll the cabinet to 1

    Where the coffee usually is, at the left edge of the lobby.

  2. 2

    Plug in the label printer

    It has two cords: its power cord goes into the large white splitter in the cabinet, and its USB cable into the back of the iMac.

  3. 3

    Plug the white splitter into the wall

    The iMac plugs into the splitter too. Plug the splitter into the outlet above the cabinet. If it doesn’t reach, use the long orange extension cord.

  4. 4

    Turn on the iMac and the label printer

    Check the printer has Dymo 30857 labels in it.

  5. 5

    Open Compassion Food Market

    From the Dock. Its window is the queue desk, with the printing panel down its right side.

  6. 6

    Sign in if it asks

    With the shared check-in account. The app fills it in when it can. If the password is missing, type it in from the team’s shared Apple Passwords and save it when the app offers. Only if that fails, use the sealed password envelope, and tell the team the seal is broken. Not Continue with Google.

  7. 7

    Wait for Online

    The printing panel says so. Leave the app open all market: closing its window only hides it, but quitting it stops printing.

Done when the queue desk is open and the printing panel says Online.

Apple Passwords first; the sealed envelope is the only paper copy. Never write it anywhere else, say it out loud, or send it. A broken seal, or anyone else seeing it, means the password gets changed and a new envelope sealed.

2

Room Display

Auditorium, back wall

  1. 1

    Roll the TV stand to 2

    Just right of the pillar as you face the back wall.

  2. 2

    Plug in its one cord

    Everything runs from one long black cord, on the back of the stand near its base. Plug it into the outlet behind the stand.

  3. 3

    Bring the keyboard and turn it on

    The Logitech K400 is in the bin in the rolling cabinet at check-in. Its trackpad is the mouse pointer.

  4. 4

    Turn on the TV and the Mac mini, and log in

    Log in using the keyboard. Team leaders know the password.

  5. 5

    Wait for the kiosk view

    It opens by itself once you’ve logged in. If not, open the browser and go to app.thebaycompassion.org/kiosk.

  6. 6

    If it asks for an unlock code

    1. On your phone, open app.thebaycompassion.org/admin/queue and sign in.
    2. Tap Unlock a room display: a button before the line opens, or under ⋮ at the top once it’s running.
    3. Type the six-digit code into the kiosk view and click Unlock. It stays unlocked for about six months.
  7. 7

    Fill the screen

    With the K400’s trackpad, click the button in the bottom-right corner of the kiosk view.

Done when the kiosk view fills the TV and faces the waiting area.

Backup Plans

If a station can’t be set up or stops working, switch to its backup and carry on. For fixes to common problems, open Help in the dashboard.

Printing doesn’t work
Write name tags by hand: the guest’s first name, last initial, and number.
The room display doesn’t work
Call numbers out loud. Guests can also see their place in line in the app.
Neither works
Run the market from the app alone. It still draws the lottery on its own; call guests from the queue desk on your phone.
The app doesn’t work
Use the deli counter tickets: hand them out, and call guests by ticket number.
