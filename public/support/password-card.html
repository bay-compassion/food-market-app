Password Envelope Cards

  1. Print at 100% (Actual Size), not scaled to fit. Cut along the dashed lines.
  2. Write the check-in account’s email and password on a card by hand. Never type the password into this page or anywhere else.
  3. Seal the card in a small envelope, put the matching label on its front, fill in the date and your name, and sign across the flap.
  4. Shred the spare cards, or keep them blank.
