Team Leader Printouts

Printed guides and templates for the team leaders who set up and run the market. Open one, then print it. The guest sign-up flyers are at /flyers.

  • Market Setup Setting up check-in and the room display before doors open, with the backup plans. Two pages; print double-sided.
  • Password Envelope Cards Cards for the check-in password, written by hand and sealed in a small envelope, with a label for its front. Four to a page.

Printing: use letter paper at 100% scale (not “fit to page”), and turn on background graphics if your browser asks, so the shaded panels print.
