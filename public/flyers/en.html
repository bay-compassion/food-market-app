Food Market

New app · Starts Sat, Oct 31

Scan to sign up

It’s a lottery now, not a race.Sign up from anywhere. Everyone has the same chance.

Every Saturday

  1. 1
    10:30 AM

    Scan the code

    Scan from anywhere, even at home. You have 15 minutes, and the screen shows how much time is left.

  2. 2

    Choose your language

    The app remembers it for next time.

  3. 3

    Fill in the form

    Enter your first and last name (please, not just initials), cell phone, and a few questions about your household. There’s no rush, so take your time. Then tap the button to enter the lottery.

  1. 4

    Tap “Text Me About Updates”

    Optional, but without it you won’t get any texts.

  2. 5
    10:45 AM

    See if you got a number

    The lottery draws and your screen shows the result and your place in line. If you got a number, head to the market. Not everyone gets a number each week.

  3. 6
    11:30 AM

    Shopping begins

    When it’s your turn, we’ll text you (if you did step 4) and your screen will say so. Come to the front door.

Please bring the phone you signed up on. We can’t accept screenshots.

Need help? Ask any volunteer.
