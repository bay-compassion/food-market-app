How It Works

  1. 1
    10:30 AM

    Scan the code

    Scan from anywhere. You have 15 minutes, so there’s no rush.

  2. 2

    Fill in the form

    Choose your language, then enter your full name, cell phone, and a few questions about your household.

  3. 3

    Tap “Click here for text updates”

    Optional, but without it you won’t get any texts.

  1. 4
    10:45 AM

    See if you got a number

    The lottery draws and your screen shows the result. Not everyone gets a number each week.

  2. 5

    Got a number? Come to the market

    Head to 4725 Evora Rd.

  3. 6
    11:30 AM

    Shopping begins

    When it’s your turn, we’ll text you (if you did step 3) and your screen will say so. Come to the check-in desk.

Please bring the phone you signed up on. We can’t accept screenshots.
