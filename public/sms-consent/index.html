SMS Consent Flow

The Bay Compassion sends customer care messages about a food market guest's queue and visit status. SMS consent is optional and is collected through a separate, affirmative website action.

How Consent Is Collected

Providing a phone number while saving information or checking in does not opt a guest in to SMS. A guest can receive food market service and follow updates in the application without consenting to text messages. A QR code may open the application, but scanning it also does not grant consent.

An identified guest must select Notify Me About Updates, check the separate consent box, and select Enable text updates. The checkbox is unchecked by default, and the submission button is disabled until the guest checks it.

  1. Step 1

    Save Information Without SMS Consent

    The guest may save their name and phone number for a future visit. This action does not enable SMS. The page provides links to the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

  2. Step 2

    Select the Separate Notification Action

    The identified guest selects Notify Me About Updates. This opens the consent dialog; it does not itself grant consent.

  3. Step 3

    Review the Disclosure

    The dialog identifies The Bay Compassion, explains the message purpose and frequency, discloses that message and data rates may apply, provides STOP and HELP instructions, and states that consent is not required for food market service.

    The checkbox is initially unchecked, and Enable text updates is disabled.

  4. Step 4

    Give Affirmative Consent

    The guest must check the consent box before Enable text updates becomes available.

  5. Step 5

    Receive Confirmation

    After submission, the application confirms that text notifications are enabled.

Program Disclosures

  • Messages concern only the guest's food market queue and visit status.
  • Message frequency varies, typically no more than a few messages per market day.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance.
  • Consent is not required to sign up for or receive food market service.
