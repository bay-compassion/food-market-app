A simpler way to welcome every neighbor.

A one-minute look at how The Bay Compassion's community food market runs its line. Guests register on their own phones in eight languages, and the day's lottery gives everyone the same chance. Volunteers then call each number from a phone at the desk, and it lights up on the room display and on the guest's phone at the same moment.

The screens are simplified recreations of the real app, with fictional guests. Turn the sound on for the soundtrack.
