# Privacy Policy

**The Bay Compassion**
Effective Date: August 14, 2026
Last Updated: August 14, 2026

The Bay Compassion ("The Bay Compassion," "we," "us," or "our") is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization serving the East Bay community through programs including Compassion Food, Compassion Health, Compassion Education, and Compassion Homeless Care. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you visit our website, use our food market guest sign-up app, volunteer with us, donate to us, or otherwise interact with our programs and services (collectively, our "Services").

By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information reasonably needed to operate our programs and communicate with the people we serve, volunteer with us, or support us.

- **Food market guests:** name, phone number, and a self-set PIN, used to verify your identity and protect your place in the guest queue. We do not require a full account or persistent login to receive food market services.
- **Volunteers:** name, contact information, availability, and background check information where required for programs involving vulnerable populations.
- **Donors:** name, contact information, and payment information processed through our third-party payment processor. We do not directly store full payment card numbers.
- **Program participants:** information relevant to the specific program you participate in, such as a child's name and reading progress, or basic intake information for health and homeless care services.
- **General contact:** information you submit through contact forms, event registration, or email correspondence.

When you visit our website, we (or our analytics and hosting providers) may also automatically collect your IP address, browser and device type, general location, pages visited, and cookies or similar technologies.

---

## 2. Sensitive Information

Some of our programs involve information that requires extra care. Volunteers working with children or vulnerable populations may be required to complete a background or fingerprint check through a third-party provider. Guests of Compassion Health or homeless care programs may share information related to their circumstances or needs; we collect only what is necessary to provide services and limit access internally to staff and volunteers directly involved in service delivery.

We do not knowingly collect Social Security numbers, government ID numbers, or financial account numbers beyond what is needed for payment processing, which is handled by our payment processor and not stored by us.

---

## 3. SMS / Text Messaging Program

If you provide your phone number to receive text messages from us, for example as a food market guest, the following applies specifically to that program.

- **What you'll receive:** queue and wait updates, market schedule changes or closures, and identity verification codes tied to your PIN. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages through this program.
- **Consent:** by providing your phone number and checking the consent box at sign-up, you agree to receive text messages related to your visit and program participation. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of receiving food, services, or program participation through any other channel.
- **Frequency:** message frequency depends on your program participation, such as weekly during active food market use.
- **Cost:** message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
- **Opt-out:** reply **STOP** at any time to unsubscribe from text messages. Reply **HELP** for assistance, or contact us using the details below.
- **Carriers:** we are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages, which can occur due to factors outside our control.

We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

---

## 4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Operate and administer our programs (food markets, education, health, homeless care)
- Verify your identity and protect your place in a service queue
- Communicate with you about your visit, appointment, or program participation
- Process donations and send donation receipts
- Coordinate volunteer scheduling and required background checks
- Improve our website and Services
- Comply with legal, tax, and reporting obligations as a nonprofit organization
- Report aggregate, de-identified impact statistics — we do not publish personally identifiable guest information in impact reporting without explicit consent

---

## 5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

- **Service providers**, including Twilio for SMS text messaging, Auth0 for identity verification and authentication, our hosting provider for secure data storage, our payment processor for donation processing, and background check providers for volunteer screening. These providers are contractually limited to using your information only to perform services on our behalf.
- **Community partners and local churches**, where a program is delivered in partnership with a local organization, limited information may be shared as needed to coordinate service delivery, consistent with the purpose you provided it for.
- **Legal requirements:** we may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety of our staff, volunteers, or the community we serve.
- **Business transfers:** if The Bay Compassion undergoes a merger, reorganization, or transfer of program operations, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy or a successor policy.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide the program or service you engaged with, meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements as a nonprofit, and resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. Food market queue and verification data is retained for a limited period after your visit and then deleted or de-identified. Donor and volunteer records may be retained longer to meet nonprofit recordkeeping and tax requirements.

---

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how visitors use our site, and support donation and volunteer sign-up forms. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

---

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

**California residents** have the right to know what personal information we have collected and how it's used, request deletion of personal information (subject to legal exceptions), correct inaccurate information, opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell personal information), and to be free from discrimination for exercising these rights.

**Regardless of location**, you may request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, withdraw consent to receive SMS communications at any time by replying STOP, and ask questions about how your information is used. We will verify your identity before processing requests involving personal information; for food market guests, this may include confirming your phone number and PIN.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

Some of our programs, such as our literacy program, serve children. In these cases we collect only the minimum information necessary, such as a first name and reading progress, and information about a minor is collected with the involvement and consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through our website or SMS program without parental involvement.

---

## 10. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted storage of sensitive fields such as PINs (which are hashed and never stored in plain text) and access limited to staff and volunteers who need it to perform their role. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected individuals as required by law.

---

## 11. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party sites, such as our payment processor, social media, or partner organizations. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

---

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated through our website or, where appropriate, directly to affected individuals.

---

## 13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact The Bay Compassion by email, phone, or through our website.
