# Text Messages

These are the messages sent to guests who enabled text updates. Phone alerts use the same titles and
mostly the same wording. The registration confirmation differs: the text says "Your entry into the
lottery has been confirmed", while the alert says "Your registration is confirmed."

The shaded parts of each example are required: `The Bay Compassion:` at the start and
`Reply STOP to unsubscribe` at the end. They remain in English in every language and cannot be edited.
The wording between them can be reviewed.
