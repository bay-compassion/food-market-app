# Text Messages

What a guest is texted, once they have agreed to text updates. Push notifications, the alerts a phone
can show, use the same titles. Their wording matches too, apart from the first message: the text says
"Your entry into the lottery has been confirmed", where the alert says "Your registration is
confirmed."

Two pieces of every text message are **required** and shaded on every message below: the sender's name
at the start, `The Bay Compassion:`, and the line `Reply STOP to unsubscribe` at the end. They are not
optional, they are in English whatever language the guest uses, and they cannot be edited. Only the
wording between them can be changed.
