# Loading and Unavailable

What a guest sees while the market is being looked up, and when it cannot be.

## Checking the market's status

The first thing the app does is ask whether a market is running. On a slow connection the guest waits
on this message.

<Still
	id="market-loading"
	alt="The app bar, and a spinner with the words 'Checking today's market status'"
/>

## The market's status could not be loaded

If the request fails, nothing tells the guest that something went wrong. They see the Market Closed
card, whatever the market's real state is.

<Still
	id="market-unavailable"
	alt="The Market Closed card, shown because the market's status could not be loaded"
/>
