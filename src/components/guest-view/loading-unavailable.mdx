# Loading and Unavailable

These examples show what happens while the app checks the market's status, and when that check fails.

## While the app checks

When a guest opens the app, it checks whether a market is running. On a slow connection, the guest
sees this loading screen.

<Still
	id="market-loading"
	alt="The app bar, and a spinner with the words 'Checking today's market status'"
/>

## If the check fails

**Decision needed.** If the app cannot reach the server, it cannot tell whether registration is open
or the market is underway. Today it shows the Market Closed card anyway. That could tell a guest to
come back next Saturday when they may be able to register now.

<Still
	id="market-unavailable"
	alt="The Market Closed card, shown because the market's status could not be loaded"
/>

Which response should the app use when it cannot check the status?

1. Explain that the status could not be checked and ask the guest to try again.
2. Show the registration form and let the server accept or reject a submission. This was the
   original intention.
3. Keep showing the Market Closed card.
