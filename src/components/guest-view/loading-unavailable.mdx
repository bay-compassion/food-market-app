# Loading and Unavailable

What a guest sees while the market is being looked up, and when it cannot be.

## Checking the market's status

The first thing the app does is ask whether a market is running. On a slow connection the guest waits
on this message.

<Still
	id="market-loading"
	alt="The app bar, and a spinner with the words 'Checking today's market status'"
/>

## The market's status could not be loaded

**Decision needed.** This is not an ordinary unavailable screen. When the app cannot reach the server
it cannot tell whether a market is running, and it shows the Market Closed card anyway. During an open
market that is false: the guest is told registration opens next Saturday when it may be open now, and
may leave rather than try again.

<Still
	id="market-unavailable"
	alt="The Market Closed card, shown because the market's status could not be loaded"
/>

The choices, for a guest whose phone cannot reach the server:

1. Say that the status could not be checked, and ask the guest to try again.
2. Show the registration form anyway, and let the server accept or refuse it. This was the original
   intention.
3. Leave it as it is.
