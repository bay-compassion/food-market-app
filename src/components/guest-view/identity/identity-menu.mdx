# Identity Menu

The menu on the "Recognized on this device" card lets a guest manage the information saved on this
phone. It also offers Opt Out when the guest has enabled text updates.

## Open the menu

<Screenshot

/>

## Show the device ID

The device ID identifies this phone to the market. It lives in the top app bar menu, next to Staff
Login, rather than on this card — it's available there once this phone has saved an identity. A team
member may ask the guest to read it aloud or copy it. The ID shown here is made up.

<Screenshot
	id="device-id"
	alt="A dialog titled Device ID, showing a made-up ID and a Copy button"
/>

## Forget saved information

This removes the saved name, phone number, and device ID from this phone. The app asks the guest to
confirm because the information cannot be recovered from the phone.
