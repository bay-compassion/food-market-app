# The Bay Compassion — review document

What a guest sees and is sent, exactly as the app shows it on a phone.

## How to read it

Every screen is shown inside the app's own top bar and footer, at the size of a phone. Screens and
messages are numbered **section.figure** — the third screen in section 2 is **2.3** — so quote the
number when you mark a page up. The margins are left empty on purpose; write in them.

Some screens are photographs of the running app rather than pictures of a single card, because they
only appear after a guest does something, such as opening a dialog. The words in the app are what is
being reviewed, not how the photograph is framed.
