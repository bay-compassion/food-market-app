Most of what we need from you is feedback on the wording: anything that is unclear, wrong, unkind, or
inconsistent. A few places also need a decision. Those are listed under Decisions to review.

## How the app works

Guests use the app on their phone to get a place in line at the market. Places are given by lottery,
not first come, first served. A guest registers during a short window, and when it closes the app
draws the lottery. Selected guests are put in line, shown their place, and called to the entrance when
it is their turn. Guests who are not selected are told so.

The screen a guest sees depends on two things.

**What the market is doing.** The market is in one of four states:

- **Market Closed**: most of the week, and again once the market is over.
- **Registration Open**: guests can register.
- **Registration Closed**: the window has shut and the lottery is being drawn.
- **Market Underway**: guests are being served.

**Where the guest's own visit stands.** A guest who registers is **Registered**, then **Waiting**
once selected, **Called** when it is their turn, and **Served** when they are done. Three other
outcomes are possible: **Not Selected**, **No Show**, and **Cancelled**.

The Guest States section shows every one of these screens and how they connect. The other sections
cover the forms, the texts a guest can choose to receive, and the QR codes staff use.

## How to read it

Every screen is shown at the size of a phone, inside the app's own top bar and footer. Screens and
text messages are numbered by section and position: the third screen in section 2 is **2.3**. Please
quote the number in your feedback. The margins are left empty for your notes.

Some screens only appear after a guest does something, such as opening a dialog. Those are
screenshots of the running app.

## About the examples

The screens are sample content in English, made for review. They are not from a live market, and some
details are made up or fixed:

- The guest is always **Sample Guest**. The phone numbers, the device ID, and the number texts come
  from are made up.
- The clock is fixed at ten on a Saturday morning, so a countdown reads the same every time. The
  queue positions shown (7th, 25th) are examples.
- Staff write the questions a market adds to the registration form and the wording of a broadcast
  text, separately for each market. The ones shown are examples.
- The web address under the QR code is a preview address, not the live site's.
- The app has translations for several other languages. After deciding on the English copy, we will
  translate it into the other languages.
