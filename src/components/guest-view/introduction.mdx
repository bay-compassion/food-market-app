This document shows what guests read in the app and in text messages. Please review the wording before
launch: flag anything unclear, inaccurate, unkind, or inconsistent. We also need your answers to the
five decisions below.

## How to review

Start with the decisions on this page, then review the screens and messages in the sections that
follow. Each example has a section and item number; **2.3** means the third item in section 2. Please
include that number with your comments. The margins are available for notes.

Screens appear at phone size, with the app's top bar and footer. Some interactions, such as an open
dialog or an error, are shown as screenshots of the running app.

The examples are in English and use sample data:

- The guest is **Sample Guest**. Phone numbers, the device ID, and the number that sends texts are
  made up.
- The clock is fixed at 10 a.m. on a Saturday, so countdowns stay the same. Queue positions such as
  7th and 25th are examples.
- Questions added to a registration form and broadcast texts are written by staff for each market.
  The versions here are examples.
- The address below the QR code is a preview address.

The app also has translations. Once the English wording is settled, the other languages will need a
copy review too.

## What guests do

Guests register on their phones during a short window to enter a lottery for a place in line.
Registering earlier does not improve their chances. After registration closes, the lottery selects
guests for the queue. Selected guests can see their place and are called to the entrance when it is
their turn. Guests who are not selected are told so.

The screen depends on whether registration is open and on the guest's own visit. A registered guest
may go from **Registered** to **Waiting**, **Called**, and **Served**. Other possible outcomes are
**Not Selected**, **No Show**, and **Cancelled**. The Guest States section shows the full set of
screens and the paths between them. The other sections cover forms, text updates, messages, and staff
QR codes.
