# Guest Forms

A guest fills in a form in one of three situations. Which one they see depends on whether this phone
already knows them, and on whether registration is open.

## Saving information for later — the Identity Form

A guest can save their name and phone number ahead of time, from the "Save my information" button
shown while registration is not open. It asks for nothing else. It does not enter the lottery and it
does not hold a place. The details are kept on this phone, so the guest does not need to enter them
again on their next visit.

<Story of={InformationForm.Default} />

Once the form is submitted, the guest is told their information is saved.

<Still id="identity-saved" alt="The identity form's success message: Your information is saved" />

## A new guest entering the lottery — the Combined Form

When registration is open and this phone does not know the guest yet, one form asks for everything at
once: name, phone number, and household details. Submitting it enters the lottery, and the name and
phone number are saved on this phone for next time.

<Story of={CombinedForm.RegistrationForm} />

## A returning guest entering the lottery — the Lottery Form

When registration is open and this phone already knows the guest, because they saved their
information or registered before, the app shows who they are and asks only for the household
details. The name and phone number are already known. The household details are asked every market,
because they describe that day's visit.
