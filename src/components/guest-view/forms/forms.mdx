# Guest Forms

The Food Market app has two forms that guests interact with.

## Identity Form

The `IdentityForm` is used to collect the user's first and last name and phone number. The user may only be asked to
fill this form in once if they choose to save their information.

<Story of={InformationForm.Default} />

## Lottery Form

The user is asked to fill out the `LotteryForm` for every market event. It includes demographic information.

<Story of={LotteryForm.Default} />

## Combined Form

If the user had chosen to save their identity information, they will be shown a combined form of the `InformationForm` and `LotteryForm`.

<Story of={CombinedForm.RegistrationForm} />
