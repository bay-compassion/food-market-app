# Forms

Guests enter the lottery when registration opens. A new guest fills in their name, phone number,
and household details together. On later visits, the phone remembers their information and the guest
checks their household details before entering again.

## Enter the lottery without saved information

During registration, a guest whose phone has no saved information enters their name, phone number,
and household details in one form. Submitting it enters the lottery and saves their name and phone
number on this phone for next time.

<Story of={CombinedForm.RegistrationForm} />

## Enter the lottery with saved information

During registration, a guest who previously registered on this phone sees
their name and enters only their household details. The app asks for those details at each market
because they describe that day's visit.

<Story of={LotteryForm.Default} />

## Describe yourself

Under the household details, a guest is asked how they describe their race and ethnicity. The
question and its seven categories follow the federal standard, so the market's counts can be
compared with Census figures when it applies for grants. A guest can tick more than one, describe
an identity in their own words, or prefer not to answer. The question is optional.

It is asked only until the guest answers. The phone remembers that the guest answered, but not
the answer, so it never appears on screen at a later market. Reports show only totals, never one
guest's answer.
