# Forms

There are three ways a guest can provide information. Saving a name and phone number prepares this
phone for a future visit; it does not enter the lottery. When registration opens, the guest enters the
lottery with either a full form or a shorter form, depending on what is already saved on the phone.

## Save information before registration opens

The "Save my information" button opens a form for the guest's name and phone number. Those details
stay on this phone for next time. Saving them does not reserve a place or enter the lottery.

After submitting, the guest sees a confirmation that their information was saved.

## Enter the lottery without saved information

During registration, a guest whose phone has no saved information enters their name, phone number,
and household details in one form. Submitting it enters the lottery and saves their name and phone
number on this phone for next time.

<Story of={CombinedForm.RegistrationForm} />

## Enter the lottery with saved information

During registration, a guest who previously saved their information or registered on this phone sees
their name and enters only their household details. The app asks for those details at each market
because they describe that day's visit.
