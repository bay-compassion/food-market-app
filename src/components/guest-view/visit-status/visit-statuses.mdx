# Guest States

Guests move through a distinct set of states when using the app. Each one is a screen of its own, and
each has a color: the same color marks its box in the chart below and its heading further down.

<Mermaid chart={guestStateFlowchart()} />

<br />

Some moments have no screen of their own, so they are arrows in the chart rather than boxes. When the
lottery is drawn, a registered guest keeps seeing the Registered card until their result arrives.
When registration closes, a guest who has already registered sees no change either.

## Status Cards

The following cards are used to represent the status of a guest.

### <StatePip state="marketClosed" /> Market Closed

This is the state for most of the week, from the time when the previous market closes until
registration opens for the next one. A guest sees this card again once the market they took part in
has closed.

<Story of={NotOpen} />

### <StatePip state="registrationOpen" /> Registration Open

A guest who has not registered yet is shown the registration form. A new guest is asked for their name
and phone number together with their household details; a guest the app already knows is asked for the
household details alone. See the forms documentation for more information.

<Story of={RegistrationForm} />

### <StatePip state="registered" /> Registered

The guest has filled out the registration form and is waiting for the lottery draw. They keep seeing
this card after registration closes, until their result arrives.

<Story of={GuestStates.Registered} />

### <StatePip state="waiting" /> Waiting

The guest was selected in the lottery. The card shows their place in line and how many guests are
ahead of them. The guest at the front of the line is told they are next.

<Story of={GuestStates.Waiting} />

<Story of={GuestStates.WaitingNext} />

### <StatePip state="called" /> Called

A worker has called the guest. The card changes to tell them it is their turn, and asks them to come to
the entrance.

<Story of={GuestStates.Called} />

### <StatePip state="served" /> Served

The guest's visit is complete.

<Story of={GuestStates.Served} />

### <StatePip state="notSelected" /> Not Selected

The guest registered but was not selected in the lottery. Supplies are limited, so not everyone can be
placed.

<Story of={GuestStates.NotPlaced} />

### <StatePip state="noShow" /> No Show

A worker called the guest but could not find them. A worker can still return them to the line until the
market closes.

<Story of={GuestStates.NoShow} />

### <StatePip state="cancelled" /> Cancelled

The guest gave up their place. A guest can only do this while they are Registered or Waiting — once
they have been called, it is a worker's decision. If registration is still open, a guest who cancels is
offered the registration form again; once it has closed they see this card until the market closes.

<Story of={GuestStates.Cancelled} />

Before giving up their place, the guest is asked to confirm.

<Still
	id="cancel-asked"
	alt="A sheet asking the guest to confirm cancelling their place in the queue, with Yes cancel and Keep my place buttons"
/>

### <StatePip state="registrationClosed" /> Registration Closed

The guest did not register, and the registration window has closed. This covers the short wait while the
lottery is being drawn, too.

<Story of={RegistrationClosed} />

### <StatePip state="marketUnderway" /> Market Underway

The guest did not register, and arrived after the market had started serving people. They are asked to
speak with a market team member.

<Story of={InProgress} />
