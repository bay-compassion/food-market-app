## Before a guest enters the lottery

These screens depend on whether the market is accepting registrations.

<div className="review-state">

### <StatePip state="marketClosed" /> Market Closed

This card appears between markets, before registration opens. It appears again after the market ends.

<Story of={NotOpen} />

</div>

<div className="review-state">

### <StatePip state="registrationOpen" /> Registration Open

A guest who has not registered sees a form. If this phone has their name and number saved, the form
asks only for today's household details. Otherwise it asks for their name and number too. See Forms
for both versions.

<Story of={RegistrationForm} />

</div>

## After a guest enters the lottery

These cards show the guest's progress from registration through the queue and service.

<div className="review-state">

### <StatePip state="registered" /> Registered

The guest has entered the lottery. This card remains after registration closes, until their result
arrives.

<Story of={GuestStates.Registered} />

</div>

<div className="review-state">

### <StatePip state="waiting" /> Waiting

The guest was selected. The card leads with their number, which never changes once given, and
shows how the line is moving under it.
Like a deli ticket, it is stamped with the guest's name and today's date beside a slowly blinking
dot, so a check-in worker can tell a live screen for today from a screenshot.

<Story of={GuestStates.Waiting} />

</div>

<div className="review-state">

### <StatePip state="called" /> Called

A team member has called the guest. The card asks them to come to the check-in desk, and carries the same
name, date, and dot as the waiting card — and the same number, on a green board, for the worker at
the check-in desk to check. The "now calling" row stays under it. A turn never expires, but others are
waiting: three minutes after the call the board starts to warm toward yellow, like the registration
countdown, faster with every minute, and at ten minutes the card says that others are waiting
behind them.

<Story of={GuestStates.Called} />

<Story of={GuestStates.CalledRunningLate} />

</div>

<div className="review-state">

### <StatePip state="served" /> Checked In

The guest has checked in at the desk, our last hand-off of the day. They still have their shopping to
do, so the card wishes them well rather than saying the visit is over.

<Story of={GuestStates.Served} />

</div>

## Other visit outcomes

Not every visit ends with service. These cards cover the other results and actions.

<div className="review-state">

### <StatePip state="notSelected" /> Not Selected

The guest entered the lottery but was not selected. Supplies are limited, so not everyone receives a
place in line.

<Story of={GuestStates.NotPlaced} />

</div>

<div className="review-state">

### <StatePip state="noShow" /> No Show

A team member called the guest but could not find them. A team member can return them to the line
before the market closes.

<Story of={GuestStates.NoShow} />

</div>

<div className="review-state">

### <StatePip state="cancelled" /> Cancelled

The guest chose to cancel while Registered or Waiting. After they are called, only a team member can
change their status. If registration is still open, the guest can enter again; after it closes, they
see this card until the market ends.

<Story of={GuestStates.Cancelled} />

</div>

## Guests who missed registration

These screens appear when a guest has not entered the lottery.

<div className="review-state">

### <StatePip state="registrationClosed" /> Registration Closed

Registration has closed, and this guest did not enter the lottery. They see this card from the
moment the window closes, through the draw, and while shopping is underway: whichever phase the
market is in, all that is left for them is next time, so the card says when that is and lays out how
a market Saturday runs.
