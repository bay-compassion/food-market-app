# QR Codes

Guests may scan one of two QR codes: a poster that opens the app, or a code from a market team member
that connects this phone to a guest they registered manually.

## Open the app from a poster

Scanning the poster opens the app. The address shown here belongs to this preview; a live poster will
show the live site's address. The Back and Print buttons appear on screen but not on the printed
poster.

**Wording to check.** The poster says "Scan to check in" and "the check-in page". Scanning opens the
app; the guest must still register to enter the lottery. See the terminology decision in the
Introduction.

## Connect a new phone to a manually registered guest

A team member can register a guest manually and show them a code. Scanning it opens this screen; the
guest must tap the button to connect their phone to that registration.

<Story of={GuestClaimCard.FreshPhone} />

## Connect a phone with saved information

If the phone already has someone else's information saved, the screen warns that connecting it to the
new registration will replace those details and that they cannot be recovered from the phone.
